An academic website and digital archive honoring the scientific career and landmark contributions of **Anuradha Mishra**, molecular geneticist and co-author of foundational research in early bovine genomics, microsatellite linkage mapping, and quantitative trait locus (QTL) analysis during the formative era of modern livestock genetics (1990–1995).

---

## 🌟 Research Highlights

- **Weaver Disease Positional Mapping (*PNAS*, 1993):** Co-discovered tight linkage between microsatellite marker `TGLA116` and bovine progressive degenerative myeloencephalopathy (weaver disease) with a maximum LOD score of 8.15. Formally designated as a **Landmark Paper** by the University of Sydney's *Online Mendelian Inheritance in Animals* (OMIA 001047-9913) database.
- **Horn Development Locus (*Nature Genetics*, 1993):** Mapped the polled (hornless) Mendelian locus in *Bos taurus* to bovine chromosome 1, establishing the scientific foundation for marker-assisted breeding of naturally hornless cattle. Also designated as an **OMIA Landmark Paper** (OMIA 000483-9913).
- **Dairy Production QTL Mapping (*Genetics*, 1995):** Large-scale statistical linkage study investigating **1,518 sires** with 159 microsatellite markers and production records from over **150,000 daughters**. Reported foundational evidence for polygenes controlling milk yield, fat, and protein on chromosomes 1, 6, 9, 10, and 20.
- **Bovine VNTR Characterization (*Genomics*, 1991):** Isolated and characterized 36 bovine Variable Number of Tandem Repeat (VNTR) markers with 59.3% mean heterozygosity, demonstrating sequence and map conservation across Bovidae.

## 🎓 Academic Trajectory

| Period | Institution | Degree / Role | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1982 – 1985** | **Rama Devi Women's Autonomous College** (Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India) | B.Sc. Biology | Foundational biological sciences with distinction |
| **1985 – 1987** | **Utkal University** (Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India) | M.Sc. Zoology | Post-graduate specialization in genetics and organismal physiology |
| **1988 – 1990** | **University of Utah** (Salt Lake City, Utah, USA) | Ph.D. Program in Molecular Biology | Doctoral training in mammalian genetics & molecular biology |
| **1990 – 1995** | **Genmark Inc.** (Salt Lake City, Utah, USA) | Research Scientist / Molecular Geneticist | Cattle genomics research team led by Dr. Michel Georges |
| **1995** | *Conscious Life Transition* | Voluntary Retirement | Dedication to raising and nurturing family |

> **Note on Career Transition (1995):**  
> In 1995, following five intensely productive and historic years that helped cement the foundations of livestock genomics, Anuradha Mishra chose to take voluntary retirement from laboratory and commercial research to dedicate herself to family life. Her 1990–1995 publications remain permanently recognized in international genomics databases and scientific reviews.

## 🔍 Academic Disambiguation

In bibliographic indexes (e.g. Google Scholar, Scopus, OpenAlex), multiple researchers share the name *Anuradha Mishra*. The researcher documented here was active in bovine molecular genomics at **Genmark Inc., Salt Lake City, Utah between 1990 and 1995**. She is distinct from Professor Anuradha Mishra in polymer and green chemistry (Gautam Buddha University) and other namesake scholars.
